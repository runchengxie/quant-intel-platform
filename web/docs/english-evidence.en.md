# English evidence presentation

The English site consumes the same public facts, dates, content hashes and approved claim sources as the Chinese site. Currency presentation must retain explicit USD/CNY units. An unspecified hundred-million unit does not establish a currency.

October 1, 2026 reviewed research has exact source-bound English entries in `US_RESEARCH_TRANSLATIONS`. They translate the unchanged Chinese claims and preserve company guidance, publication context and scheduled-versus-released distinctions. USD revenue is displayed in billions with the corresponding scale conversion. These entries do not approve new news or replace canonical evidence. Changed or unknown future prose is retained rather than assumed to match an existing entry. Complete paragraphs are translated before SVG line wrapping.

The English homepage includes price and reviewed-claim source links, a generation timestamp in `America/New_York`, current U.S./Asia PNG controls and recent U.S. image history. Image downloads use the displayed SVG and canonical report identity. Source Markdown remains available for evidence review.

The public research projection currently retains event IDs and source URLs, not individual structured source timestamps. Dates present in the approved prose remain visible. Future structured timestamp publication requires a separately tested public contract change and a new reviewed artifact, not inferred metadata or private review-file exposure.

Static-site publication does not switch production runtimes, fetch prices, call models, change personal delivery receipts or send messages.
